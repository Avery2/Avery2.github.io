Accessibility

Accessibility asks whether different people can perceive and operate the same meaning.

AI-generated exampleThis is substantive prototype content written to test the linked-reading interface, not Avery’s published writing.

Accessibility is not a final compliance pass over an otherwise finished design. It changes the design’s model of perception and action from the beginning.

Meaning should survive variation: color-vision differences, zoom, keyboard input, screen readers, reduced motion, narrow viewports, and temporary fatigue. That favors native links, semantic headings, robust contrast, and interfaces whose structure does not depend on animation.
